# HumanID World-Class Product Plan

**Branch**: strategy/humanid/world-class-plan
**Date**: February 20, 2026
**Status**: Awaiting CEO review

## Summary

Comprehensive strategic plan to make HumanID a world-class digital identity
platform. Covers 3-year roadmap across 6 horizons from foundation through
market leadership.

## Key Strategic Decisions

1. **The One-Line Strategy**: "Be the Stripe of digital identity"
2. **Developer-led growth** (DLG) as primary GTM motion
3. **No token** -- avoid crypto regulatory risk and enterprise friction
4. **Open-source core** -- ZK circuits and DID libraries open, platform proprietary
5. **Education vertical first** -- universities as first issuers (lowest friction)
6. **EUDI interoperability by Nov 2026** -- ride the EU mandate wave

## Market Context

- Digital identity market: $47B (2025), $132B (2031)
- Decentralized identity: $3B (2025), growing at 65-70% CAGR
- EU wallet mandate: December 2026 (450M citizens)
- W3C VC 2.0: Ratified standard (May 2025)
- FIDO2 passkeys: 69% consumer adoption, 87% enterprise

## Competitive Gap Exploited

No competitor simultaneously delivers:
- W3C VC 2.0 compliance
- ZKP selective disclosure
- Biometric proofing without special hardware
- Developer-first API (Stripe-level DX)
- No token dependency
